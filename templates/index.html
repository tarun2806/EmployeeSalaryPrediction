How the model works

  1. 1 · You enter job title, experience, city, seniority and industry.
  2. 2 · Input is mapped to the saved preprocessing buckets.
  3. 3 · Three quantile models estimate P10, P50 and P90 annual salaries.
  4. 4 · The app shows the estimated salary and market range.
